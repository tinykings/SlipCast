SlipCast

A podcast player for iPhone that can skip the words you’d rather not hear. You make a list, turn it on for the shows you want, and SlipCast transcribes episodes on your phone and cuts those moments out of playback.

For iPhone, iOS 26.1 or later · Source on GitHub

What it does

Word filtering
Add your own words and phrases and enable filtering per show. SlipCast finds matches and skips them while you listen. You can widen the padding around each match if the edges come through.
Transcripts
Read along with the publisher’s transcript when there is one, or generate your own on a supported device. Tap a line to jump to that point in the episode.
Finding shows
Search Apple’s podcast directory, browse the charts, or paste in an RSS feed URL.
Downloads
Save episodes for offline listening. New episodes can download automatically, Wi‑Fi only if you like, and played ones can be cleaned up for you.
Playback
Playback speed, an Up Next queue, a sleep timer, and chapters when the show provides them. SlipCast also detects music sections, so you can jump between them to find segment breaks.
iCloud sync
Subscriptions, played history, playback positions, muted words, and most settings sync through your own iCloud account. Downloads and generated transcripts stay on each device.

Filtering and generated transcripts need Apple Speech support for your device and language, and an episode has to finish processing before you can play it filtered. Detection isn’t perfect: it can miss a word or clip something it shouldn’t. Please don’t rely on it to make an episode safe for kids.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Short version: SlipCast has no developer-operated backend, advertising, or analytics SDKs. Audio analysis runs on your device. Your app data stays on your device or syncs through your own iCloud account. Using the podcast directory and downloading content involves direct requests to Apple and podcast hosting services.

This policy covers the SlipCast iPhone app and this informational website. SlipCast is developed by tinykings. You can inspect the app’s behavior in its source code.

Information collected by the developer

SlipCast does not send your subscriptions, listening history, muted words, audio, transcripts, or personal identifiers to the developer. The app contains no analytics, third-party crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. You do not need to create a SlipCast account.

Apple may provide developers with App Store statistics or crash diagnostics according to your Apple privacy and sharing settings. These are handled by Apple, not an in-app tracking service.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your subscriptions and episode metadata, downloaded audio, transcripts, audio-analysis results, playback positions, played history, muted words and phrases, settings, and cached artwork. These support playback, filtering, and offline use. Downloaded audio and generated transcripts are not uploaded by SlipCast for processing.

On-device transcription and filtering

SlipCast uses Apple’s on-device Speech framework and bundled audio-analysis models to process podcast files. It does not record your microphone or send podcast audio to the developer or a remote transcription service. iOS may download language assets from Apple to enable on-device transcription. If a publisher provides a transcript, the app can download that transcript directly from the publisher’s host.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, SlipCast uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to sync subscriptions, per-show preferences, played history, playback positions, muted words, and supported settings between devices using your Apple Account. This can also restore these items after a reinstall. Downloads, generated transcripts, and audio scans are not included in this sync.

This data is held in your iCloud account, not on a SlipCast server, and is not accessible to the developer. Apple handles iCloud under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can manage iCloud access and storage through Apple’s system settings where available.

Services the app connects to

  • Apple’s podcast directory: searches, charts, and show lookups connect to Apple. Search text is sent to Apple to return results.
  • Podcast publishers and their hosting providers: the app requests RSS feeds, episode audio, artwork, and available transcripts and chapter files directly from the URLs supplied by those feeds or the directory. Requests may follow redirects to hosting or content delivery services.
  • Apple system services: iCloud sync and any required speech-language downloads connect to Apple.
  • Links you open: external websites handle your visit under their own privacy policies.

These connections reveal your IP address and ordinary request information to the receiving service. Podcast hosts may log downloads or use their own measurement systems. SlipCast does not control those services’ data practices; consult Apple’s and the relevant publishers’ privacy policies.

Notifications

If you enable download notifications, SlipCast asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications when new episodes finish downloading. This does not require a developer-operated push notification server. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.

Your choices and deleting data

You can remove downloaded episodes, unsubscribe from shows, and delete muted terms in the app. Subscription and muted-term changes sync to iCloud when available. Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not necessarily delete synced iCloud data or device backups; synced items may return after reinstallation. Manage any retained iCloud data or backups through Apple’s account and storage controls. Removing a download alone does not erase its played history.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, advertising, third-party fonts, tracking scripts, or site-set cookies. GitHub may log technical information, including visitor IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Tracking and sharing

SlipCast does not track you across apps or websites, sell your personal information, or share listening data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

SlipCast does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Word filtering is an automated listening aid, not a parental-control system or a guarantee of age-appropriate content.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published here with a revised effective date. Previous versions can be found in the page’s change history.

Contact

Support
